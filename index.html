04 — Built for Vegas

The desert is hard on cars.

Sun, dust and hard water are part of life in Las Vegas. Regular detailing helps your car keep up with all three.

Sun

Strong sun can fade paint and dry out dashboards, leather and trim over time.

Dust

Fine desert dust settles into vents, seams, carpets and every corner of the cabin.

Hard water

Mineral-heavy water can leave spots on glass and paint if it's left to dry.
